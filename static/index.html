How to use

The MEDUSA 2026 pipeline in 7 steps, on a single FT-ICR scan.

  1. Load — pick an mzXML from the server folder or upload your own (only the first scan of the file is used).
  2. Deisotoping — groups peaks into isotopic distributions and predicts the charge of every ion. Turn on ions on spectrum to mark each ion's peak top with a colored dot; click a dot, a peak, or a legend entry to select the ion (auto-zoom to its window). The base (highest) peak of the ion is shown in its annotation.
  3. Element classification — the Transformer estimates, for each ion, the probability that the selected element (default Ir) is present in the molecule.
  4. Threshold — the "knee" of the probability distribution (sorted descending) gives an automatic threshold; the slider overrides it manually.
  5. Highlight — Apply threshold marks the points of ions with P above the threshold in red; Zoom to highlighted ions jumps to that region.
  6. Molecular formula — select the ion (table or peak), set element limits (or pick a preset) and the mass tolerance (ppm), press Run. The ranked table is sorted by cosine similarity; CSV exports it, clicking a row sends the formula to step 7.
  7. Compare with spectrum — overlays the calculated isotopic pattern of the chosen formula on the experimental window: Δ (ppm), cosine distance and matched peaks % are shown in the top-right box.

Zoom: drag a rectangle or use the mouse wheel. Warnings (e.g. skipped ions) appear in the Log. New session clears all results. A 409 "job slot busy" means another heavy step is running — wait a few seconds and press Run again.
